# NNNN. Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** role(s), not necessarily named individuals

## Context

The forces at play: what problem or pressure prompted a decision, the constraints, and
anything a future reader needs to understand *why a choice was even required*. State facts,
not the conclusion.

## Decision

The choice made, stated plainly and in the active voice ("We use X.").

## Alternatives considered

The options that were genuinely weighed and why each was not chosen. This is the most
valuable part of an ADR — it stops a future contributor from re-treading rejected ground.

- **Alternative A** — why not.
- **Alternative B** — why not.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, or constrained as a result — positive and negative, honestly.
Include what this decision commits future work to, and what would have to change to reverse
it.

## Related

Links to the Charter rule this produces, the architecture doc that explains the resulting
mechanism, and any superseding/superseded ADRs.
